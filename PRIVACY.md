# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 15 November 2026. Plain-language template for an independent student project. Have it reviewed by a qualified person before relying on it legally, and fill in the details in square brackets.*

## Summary

- You can use the planner **without an account**. Your missions then stay in your browser and are not sent to us.
- We do **not** use advertising, tracking or analytics.
- If you create an account, we store the data you give us so the cloud library, sharing and chat can work.

## Who is responsible

InfotronX Horizon, an independent student team. Contact: [contact email].

## What happens without an account

Your missions, routes, notes and settings are saved in your browser's local storage. We never receive them. See the [Cookies and local storage](COOKIES.md) page.

## What the site loads from other services

When you open Mars Atlas your browser contacts these services, which can see your IP address and browser details. We do not control them.

- **NASA Mars Trek** (trek.nasa.gov): map imagery tiles.
- **Google Fonts** (fonts.googleapis.com): the typefaces.
- **jsDelivr** (cdn.jsdelivr.net) and **esm.sh**: the 3D library and the account library.
- **Our hosting provider** (Netlify or Vercel): serves the site and keeps standard server logs.

## If you create an account

We use **Supabase** to provide sign-in and storage. Depending on what you use, we store:

- your email address and sign-in details;
- the missions you save to the cloud, with their routes, waypoints and notes, and who you share them with;
- an optional Crew Profile: callsign, mission role, interests, biography and an optional profile image. Roles are what you type; they are not agency credentials;
- team chat messages in shared missions, and reports or blocks you make.

Access is limited with row-level security, so you and the people you share with can see a mission and others cannot. Staff can access data only to run, secure and support the service.

## Optional AI features

The Mission Copilot has a local mode that sends nothing. An AI explanation is only requested when you ask for it, and it sends the mission details needed for that answer to an AI service run by our server. Do not include personal data in mission notes.

## Why we use data

To run the planner and the cloud library, to keep the service secure, and to answer your messages. Where the law requires a legal basis, it is our legitimate interest in running the service, and your consent for optional features.

## How long we keep it

Cloud data stays until you delete it or delete your account. Server logs are kept by the hosting provider for a short period.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR) you can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, object to its use, and complain to your data protection authority. Write to [contact email]. You can delete local data any time by clearing your browser's site data.

## Children

Mars Atlas is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## Security

We use HTTPS, row-level security and do not store secrets in the code. No service is perfectly secure.

## Changes

We will update this page and its date when something changes.
